## Key Features

Label archiving is a new capability that allows users to archive labels that are no longer needed or are duplicates. This feature enables teams to manage their label lists more effectively, reducing clutter and making it easier to find the right label. Label archiving also helps teams to keep track of their label usage, making it easier to identify trends and patterns. This feature is particularly useful for teams that frequently update their labels or add new ones. By archiving unused labels, teams can save space and reduce the administrative burden associated with managing their label lists.

## Use Cases

In a scenario where a team is working on a complex project, label archiving is used to archive labels that are no longer relevant or are duplicates. This feature helps the team to maintain a clean and organized label list, reducing clutter and making it easier to find the right label. As a result, the team is able to focus on more critical tasks and deliver high-quality results, rather than spending time searching for the correct label.
